## Approved Core Verbs (Quick Reference)

These common verbs are approved in STE with explicit, unambiguous meanings:

- `add`, `adjust`, `allow`, `apply`, `attach`, `clean`, `clear`, `close`, `connect`, `cut`, `do`, `fill`, `get`, `give`, `go`, `hold`, `keep`, `know`, `let`, `make`, `move`, `open`, `pull`, `push`, `put`, `read`, `remove`, `replace`, `run`, `save`, `see`, `send`, `set`, `show`, `start`, `stop`, `test`, `try`, `turn`, `use`, `write`.

---

## Approved Core Connectors & Prepositions

- **Connectors**: `and`, `but`, `or`, `if`, `then`, `because`, `before`, `after`, `when`, `while`.
- **Prepositions**: `about`, `above`, `across`, `after`, `against`, `along`, `among`, `around`, `at`, `before`, `behind`, `below`, `beside`, `between`, `by`, `down`, `for`, `from`, `in`, `into`, `near`, `of`, `off`, `on`, `onto`, `out`, `over`, `through`, `to`, `towards`, `under`, `until`, `up`, `with`, `without`.
